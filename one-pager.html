Margin For department chairs

Teach better. Grow faster.

Margin helps every teacher in your department manage their teaching and grow, every trimester. Teachers see what their students actually learned and move toward promotion; the department gets CLO results and accreditation-ready course files without chasing anyone.

Moments teachers remember

Our privacy promise: your notes, promises and goals are never visible to the university. The department sees operational status only, such as grading counts and course-file progress. No teacher is ranked. This is built into the data model and written into the contract.

The ask: one department, one trimester

Run a pilot of Margin in your department next trimester.

  • We do: set everything up from your syllabi and the portal's Excel exports, with no integration; connect university Google or Microsoft sign-in; run a one-hour faculty workshop; give you the live department view.
  • You do: introduce us to IQAC and IT, and invite 8 to 25 teachers to try it.
  • We measure together: 30 of 50 teachers active weekly, 70% of AI drafts approved with light edits, the full CLO loop run in 5 courses, and your decision to continue.
  • Price after the pilot: 999 tk per teacher a month, paid by the department or university (or 9,990 tk a year upfront).
  • Guarantee: if 30 of 50 teachers aren't using it weekly by the end of the trimester, you don't pay.
