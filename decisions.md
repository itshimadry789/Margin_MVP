## 2026-11-02 · Sharing approve, edit and dismiss counts is opt-in and off by default
- Decided by: Vigil, default; the designer has not answered
- Why: the counts become training data for Margin's own models, and teachers were never told. Intent's ethical defaults put consent before collection.
- Rejected: on by default with a disclosure, because a teacher who never opens the privacy sheet would still be counted
- Rests on: evaluation-margin.md, "Training use of teachers' decisions is not disclosed to them"; context-margin.md, Ethical stance
